# Firmware & Copyright Notes

This repository documents the **Magcubic HY300 Pro / Allwinner H726** recovery investigation.

## Recommended public content

- Device identification
- Hardware photos and markings
- Firmware metadata
- SHA-256 hashes
- Recovery notes
- Diagnostic scripts
- Official/vendor firmware links

## Manufacturer firmware

Firmware may be copyrighted or subject to distribution restrictions.

Before committing an image to this public repository, verify that redistribution is permitted by the manufacturer or rights holder.

### Preferred workflow

1. Keep the original manufacturer image unchanged locally.
2. Record the exact filename and size.
3. Calculate SHA-256.
4. Publish the metadata/hash.
5. Link to the official/vendor source.

> **Rule:** Do not label an image "official", "correct", or "known-good" until its provenance and hardware compatibility have been verified.
